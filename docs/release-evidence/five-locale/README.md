## hhc-web production evidence

- Performance used cold mobile Lighthouse with simulated slow 4G, 4× CPU slowdown, and medians of three runs. The pre-release `zh-Hant` median LCP was 1299.2 ms. The immediate post-release series was unstable, with transient element-render-delay behavior and medians of 1639 ms (`zh-Hant`), 4262 ms (`ja`), and 4600 ms (`ko`). Stabilized three-run medians were: `zh-Hant` 1169 ms LCP / 0.010329 CLS; `ja` 1037 ms / 0.006210; `ko` 1126 ms / 0.010227; the median performance score was 100 for each locale. `zh-Hant` and `ko` each retained one slower repeat, so the immediate series is not presented as a stable locale-specific regression and the stabilized figures do not claim every individual run scored 100.
- Direct loads and client navigation covered home, About, and Literature Ministry in `zh-Hant`, `ja`, and `ko`. No unused-preload console warning, global Loading screen, font-CDN request, or mixed fallback glyph was observed.
- The production pages had no hero/control overflow at 320 px or 200%-equivalent zoom. Selector and footer placement were also checked under forced colors, dark mode, and reduced motion.
- At observation time, public latest weekly issue `1733` exposed content only for `zh-Hant` and `zh-Hans`; the English edition was not present. This is a content-availability observation. Weekly papers remain the independent three-edition domain (`zh-Hant`, `zh-Hans`, `en`), and no Japanese/Korean PDF edition is expected.
